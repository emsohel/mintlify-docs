AddtoZ's analytics dashboard gives you a real-time view of your campaign's reach and spend — helping you understand what's working and where to optimize.

## Accessing analytics

Navigate to **Campaigns → [Campaign Name] → Analytics** to open the analytics dashboard for a specific campaign.

At the top of the dashboard, you'll see four overview metrics at a glance:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total Impressions** | Cumulative estimated views across all billboards in the campaign |
| **Total Spend** | Total amount invoiced for the campaign to date |
| **Active Bookings** | Number of billboard slots currently live |
| **Days Remaining** | Days left until the campaign end date |

## Metrics explained

The analytics dashboard tracks the following metrics across your campaign:

- **Impressions** — Estimated total views based on billboard traffic data sourced from operators.
- **Reach** — Estimated number of unique viewers who have seen your campaign.
- **Spend** — Total amount invoiced for this campaign, aggregated across all bookings.
- **CPM (Cost per thousand impressions)** — Calculated as Spend ÷ (Impressions ÷ 1,000). Use this to compare the efficiency of different placements.
- **Bookings** — The number of active billboard slots included in this campaign.

## Performance by billboard

Click the **By Billboard** tab to break down impressions and spend at the individual placement level. Each row in the table shows the billboard name, location, format, impressions delivered, and spend to date.

Use this view to compare which locations are delivering the most value — and to inform decisions about where to invest more heavily in future campaigns.

## Date range filters

Use the date range selector at the top of the analytics dashboard to filter all metrics to a specific time window. This is especially useful for:

- Reviewing performance during a promotional peak (e.g., a product launch week)
- Comparing month-over-month results for long-running campaigns
- Isolating the impact of a newly added billboard booking

Select a preset range (Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This Month) or define a custom start and end date.

## Exporting reports

Click **Export** in the top-right corner of the analytics dashboard to download a CSV file containing your campaign's analytics data. The export includes all metrics and billboard-level breakdowns for the selected date range.

Use exported reports to:

- Share campaign performance with clients or stakeholders
- Import data into your own reporting or BI tools
- Archive campaign results for future reference

<Note>
  Impression data is updated daily. There may be a 24–48 hour delay before new data appears in the analytics dashboard.
</Note>
