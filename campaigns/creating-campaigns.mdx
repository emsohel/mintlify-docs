This guide walks you through creating a campaign in AddtoZ — from naming it and setting dates to adding billboard bookings and submitting artwork.

## Create a new campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Campaigns">
    Click **Campaigns** in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Campaign">
    Click **+ New Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter campaign details">
    Fill in the following fields:

    - **Campaign Name** (required)
    - **Start Date**
    - **End Date**
    - **Budget** (optional)
    - **Objective** — choose from Brand Awareness, Promotion, Event, or Other
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save as Draft">
    Click **Save Draft** to save your campaign without publishing it yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add billboards to your campaign

From the campaign detail page, click **Browse Billboards** to open the interactive billboard map. Explore available locations and click any billboard pin to view its details, pricing, and availability. When you find a billboard you want to include, click **Add to Campaign** to create a booking for that location within your campaign.

Repeat this process to add as many billboard bookings as your campaign requires. Each booking is tracked individually, with its own date range and status.

## Upload artwork

Once you have added billboard bookings to your campaign, you need to upload compliant artwork for each placement. For full instructions on accepted file formats, dimensions, and the review process, see [Uploading Billboard Artwork](/billboards/media-upload).

## Launch your campaign

Your campaign goes live automatically when all of the following conditions are met:

- All billboard bookings have been **approved** by their respective operators
- All uploaded artwork has been **accepted**
- Your campaign's **start date** has been reached

When these conditions are satisfied, the campaign status changes to **Active** and your ads begin displaying on the scheduled start date.

## Edit or pause a campaign

**Editing a campaign:** While your campaign is in **Draft** status, you can edit any campaign detail — including the name, dates, budget, and objective — by clicking **Edit** on the campaign detail page.

**Pausing an Active campaign:** To temporarily halt a running campaign, click **Pause Campaign** on the campaign detail page. Pausing stops any future bookings from activating but does not cancel bookings that are already in progress.

<Note>
  Campaigns must have at least one approved booking before they can go live. Campaigns in Draft status are not visible to the public.
</Note>
