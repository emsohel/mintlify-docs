AddtoZ gives you flexible scheduling controls so you can define exactly when and where your campaign runs — from simple date ranges to hourly time slots on digital displays.

## Campaign flight dates

Every campaign has a **Start Date** and an **End Date** that define its overall flight window:

- **Start Date** — The date your campaign goes live (provided all bookings are approved and artwork is accepted).
- **End Date** — The date your campaign ends and all active displays stop.

If you update the flight dates on an **Active** campaign, the changes take effect immediately. Extending the end date keeps the campaign running longer; moving it earlier will end the campaign sooner than originally scheduled.

## Booking date ranges

Each billboard booking within a campaign has its own independent date range, which must fall within the overall campaign flight window. Because bookings can have different start and end dates, you can:

- **Stagger placements** — roll out billboards progressively over your campaign period
- **Test different markets** — run short-burst bookings in specific locations before committing to a longer run

To adjust a booking's dates, open the campaign detail page, select the booking, and click **Edit Booking**.

## Time-of-day scheduling (Digital billboards)

For digital (LED) billboards, you can target specific display time slots rather than running your ad around the clock. The available time slots are:

| Slot | Hours |
|------|-------|
| Morning | 6 AM – 12 PM |
| Afternoon | 12 PM – 6 PM |
| Evening | 6 PM – 12 AM |
| Overnight | 12 AM – 6 AM |

Select one or more time slots when configuring your digital billboard booking. Note that time slot availability depends on the individual billboard operator's configuration — not all operators support time-of-day scheduling.

## Minimum booking duration

Some billboard operators require a minimum booking duration (for example, 7 days) before they accept a reservation. Where applicable, this requirement is displayed on the billboard's listing page. The booking calendar automatically enforces the minimum and will not allow you to select a date range shorter than the required duration.

If you need a shorter booking than a billboard's minimum allows, consider searching for a comparable billboard without that restriction.

<Tip>
  Book billboard slots at least 1–2 weeks before your campaign start date to allow time for artwork review and operator approval.
</Tip>
