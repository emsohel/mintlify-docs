AddtoZ helps you identify the right billboard locations for your target audience using geographic filters, demographic overlays, and traffic data.

## Geographic targeting

Use the map search on the **Billboards** page to find available placements in your desired area. You can narrow your search in several ways:

- **City, state, or ZIP code** — Type a location into the search bar to center the map on that area.
- **Custom region** — Click the draw tool on the map and outline a custom boundary. AddtoZ will display all available billboards within your drawn area.

Pan and zoom the map to explore results, and click any billboard pin to view its details.

## Billboard format filtering

Use the filter panel alongside the billboard directory to narrow results by:

- **Format** — Static, Digital, or Backlit
- **Size** — Filter by face dimensions to match your artwork specifications
- **Price range** — Set a minimum and maximum to stay within budget

Combining format and price filters helps you quickly surface the placements that fit both your creative requirements and your spend targets.

## Audience demographics

Some billboard listings include operator-provided audience data to help you evaluate a location's reach:

- **Estimated daily impressions** — The average number of times the billboard is seen per day
- **Age distribution** — Breakdown of the primary age groups passing the location
- **Income range** — Household income profile of the surrounding area
- **Commuter traffic** — Indication of whether the location captures heavy commuter or pedestrian flow

Use these metrics to compare locations side by side and prioritize placements with the strongest match to your target audience profile.

## Comparing billboards

To evaluate multiple billboards at once, use the **Compare** feature:

1. Browse the billboard map or directory and tick the checkbox on each billboard you want to evaluate (up to 3).
2. Click **Compare** in the comparison tray that appears at the bottom of the screen.
3. Review the side-by-side table showing **price**, **impressions**, **format**, and **location** for each selected billboard.

Use the comparison view to make a confident, data-informed decision before adding bookings to your campaign.

## Saving a shortlist

If you are not ready to book immediately, save promising billboards for later by clicking the **heart icon** on any billboard listing. Shortlisted billboards are collected under **Billboards → My Shortlist**, where you can review them at any time and convert them into bookings when you are ready.

<Info>
  Demographic and impression data is provided by billboard operators and AddtoZ does not independently verify it. Use it as a guide, not a guarantee.
</Info>
