After signing up, take a few minutes to configure your AddtoZ account so it's ready for day-to-day use. This page covers everything from filling in your business profile and adding a payment method to inviting team members and managing your notification preferences.

## Your Profile

To update your profile, go to **Settings → Profile**. Fill in the following details and click **Save Changes** when done:

- **Business name** — the trading name that appears on all client-facing documents
- **Phone number** — a contact number clients and the AddtoZ team can reach you on
- **Website** — your company website URL (optional but recommended)
- **Logo** — upload a PNG or JPG of your company logo (recommended size: 400 × 400 px)

Your profile information appears on invoices and client communications, so keep it accurate and up to date.

## Billing Information

Before you can accept paid bookings, you must add at least one payment method. Go to **Settings → Billing → Add Payment Method** and follow the on-screen prompts.

AddtoZ accepts the following payment methods:

- **Credit and debit cards** — Visa, Mastercard, and American Express
- **Bank transfer** — add your bank account details for direct ACH or wire payments

<Note>
  Billing information is required before you can accept paid bookings. AddtoZ will prompt you to complete this step if you attempt to confirm a booking without a payment method on file.
</Note>

## Team Members

You can invite colleagues to your AddtoZ workspace so they can help manage billboards, campaigns, and bookings. Go to **Settings → Team → Invite Member**, enter the person's email address, assign them a role, and click **Send Invite**. They will receive an email with instructions to join your workspace.

AddtoZ offers two roles:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Admin** | Full access to all features, including billing settings and team management |
| **Member** | Can manage billboards, campaigns, and bookings, but cannot access billing or team settings |

Assign the **Admin** role only to trusted team members who need oversight of financial and organisational settings.

## Notification Preferences

Control which email notifications AddtoZ sends you by going to **Settings → Notifications**. Toggle each notification type on or off according to your preferences:

- **New booking requests** — receive an alert whenever a client requests a booking on one of your billboards
- **Booking confirmations** — get confirmation when a booking is approved and locked in
- **Payment received** — be notified when a payment clears for a booking
- **Campaign going live** — receive a reminder when a campaign's start date arrives
- **Campaign ending soon** — get an advance notice before a campaign's end date

<Tip>
  Complete your profile fully before adding billboards — clients see your business name and contact info on every booking confirmation.
</Tip>
