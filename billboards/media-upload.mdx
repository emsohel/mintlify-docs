AddtoZ lets advertisers upload creative artwork directly to their bookings, giving operators a single place to review and approve all creative assets before a campaign goes live. This keeps communication centralised and creates a clear record of what was approved for each booking.

## Supported file formats

Upload artwork in the formats listed below. Requirements vary by billboard type.

**Static billboards**
- JPEG, PNG, PDF
- Print-ready files at **300 DPI** recommended
- Provide full-bleed artwork where required by the operator

**Digital billboards**
- MP4 (H.264 codec, maximum 30 seconds)
- GIF
- PNG sequence

**Backlit billboards**
- JPEG, PNG, PDF
- **300 DPI** recommended
- Design colours to account for back-illumination

**Maximum file size:** 100 MB per file.

## How to upload artwork

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking">
    Go to **Bookings**, find the approved booking in your list, and click it to open the booking detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Artwork tab">
    Click the **Artwork** tab in the booking detail page. This tab displays all artwork previously uploaded for this booking as well as any revision notes from the operator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    Click **Upload Artwork**, select your file from your device, and click **Upload**. The file will appear in the Artwork tab once the upload is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for review">
    The operator will review your artwork and either approve it or request revisions. You'll receive an email notification as soon as the operator acts on your submission. If revisions are requested, return to the **Artwork** tab to read the operator's notes and upload a revised file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Artwork review (operators)

As an operator, you receive an email notification each time an advertiser uploads artwork to one of your bookings. To review it:

1. Open the booking from **Bookings → [Booking Name]** and click the **Artwork** tab.
2. Preview the uploaded file directly in the browser, or download it for a closer review.
3. Choose one of the following actions:
   - **Approve** — Marks the artwork as accepted. The advertiser is notified and the campaign is cleared to run on the scheduled dates.
   - **Request Revision** — Enter a note explaining what needs to change (e.g. incorrect dimensions, low resolution, missing bleed). The advertiser is notified and prompted to upload a corrected file.

All approval actions and revision notes are logged in the booking's Artwork tab for your records.

<Tip>
  Always check the artwork specifications in the billboard listing before uploading — each operator may have specific bleed, resolution, or color profile requirements.
</Tip>
