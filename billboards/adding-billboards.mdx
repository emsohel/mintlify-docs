This guide walks billboard operators through adding new billboard listings to AddtoZ, from entering basic details to publishing inventory for advertisers.

## Before you begin

Before adding a billboard, make sure you have completed your operator profile and entered your billing information. See [Account Setup](/account-setup) for instructions.

## Add a new billboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billboards section">
    Click **Billboards** in the left navigation sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Billboard">
    Click the **+ Add Billboard** button in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter billboard details">
    Fill in the following fields:

    - **Name** (required) — A clear, identifiable name for the billboard.
    - **Description** — A short summary of the placement and audience.
    - **Format** — Select **Static**, **Digital**, or **Backlit**.
    - **Dimensions** — Enter the width × height of the display face.
    - **Facing direction** — The compass direction traffic approaches from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the location">
    Enter the full street address in the location field. The map will auto-pin the location based on the address. Drag the pin to adjust its position if the auto-placed pin is not accurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set pricing">
    Enter the base **Price per day** for the billboard. Optionally, set a minimum booking duration (in days) to prevent very short bookings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload photos">
    Upload at least one photo of the billboard. Photos are shown to advertisers browsing your inventory and are a key factor in booking decisions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Toggle **Status** to **Active** and click **Save**. Your billboard is now visible to advertisers and available to book.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Billboard formats

AddtoZ supports three billboard formats. Each format has different artwork requirements, so make sure advertisers know which format they are booking.

- **Static** — A fixed printed display. Artwork is a single print-ready file (JPEG, PNG, or PDF). Typical specs call for 300 DPI at full bleed.
- **Digital (LED)** — A programmable LED screen that can display animated or rotating content. Artwork can be video (MP4), GIF, or image sequences. Resolution and pixel pitch vary by screen — check the listing for exact specs.
- **Backlit** — A printed vinyl or film face illuminated from behind. Artwork requirements are similar to static but colours need to account for back-lighting. PDF or high-resolution PNG at 300 DPI is recommended.

## Editing and deactivating

**To edit a billboard**, go to **Billboards**, click the billboard name to open its detail page, then click **Edit**. Update any fields and click **Save** to apply your changes.

**To deactivate a billboard** without deleting it, open the billboard detail page, click **Edit**, toggle **Status** to **Inactive**, and click **Save**. The billboard is hidden from advertisers but all its data and booking history is preserved. Set it back to **Active** at any time to re-publish it.

<Tip>
  Add multiple photos showing the billboard from different angles and times of day — advertisers use these to evaluate visibility and audience.
</Tip>
