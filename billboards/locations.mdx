Accurate location data helps advertisers find your billboards and helps AddtoZ display them correctly on the map browser. Take a few extra moments to verify your pin placement — a misplaced pin can cause advertisers to overlook your listing entirely.

## Setting a location

AddtoZ gives you two ways to set a billboard's location:

- **Address search (recommended)** — Type the full street address into the location field. AddtoZ automatically geocodes the address and drops a pin on the map. This is the fastest method and works for most street-level addresses.
- **Manual pin drag** — If the auto-placed pin is not in the right position, click and drag the pin to the exact location on the map. This is useful for billboards set back from the road or on large properties where the address alone is not precise enough.

After setting the location with either method, check the confirmation map preview that appears below the address fields. Verify that the pin sits on or immediately adjacent to the billboard's actual position before saving.

## Location fields

Fill in all relevant location fields to improve your billboard's visibility in geographic searches:

- **Street address** — The full mailing address of the billboard or its host property.
- **City / State / ZIP** — Used for geographic filtering when advertisers search by area.
- **Country** — Defaults to the country set in your account settings. Update this if you operate billboards across borders.
- **Facing direction** — Select **North**, **South**, **East**, or **West** to indicate the direction passing traffic faces when viewing the billboard. This affects visibility context for advertisers.
- **Nearby landmarks** — Optional free-text notes (e.g. "Corner of Main St and 5th Ave, opposite City Hall") to help advertisers visually identify the location.

## Map view

The **Billboards** page includes an interactive map that all advertisers can use to browse inventory by location. When you set a precise pin, your billboard appears at the correct position in map searches, making it easier for advertisers targeting a specific neighbourhood or corridor to find and book it.

Billboards with inaccurate or missing pins may not surface in location-based searches, even if the city and ZIP fields are filled in correctly. Always confirm your pin position to maximise discoverability.

<Note>
  If your billboard is on a private road or new development not in the geocoding database, drag the map pin manually to the correct position.
</Note>
