The availability calendar shows which dates are booked, blocked, or open for each billboard, preventing double-bookings and helping you plan maintenance windows. Keep the calendar up to date so advertisers only request dates you can genuinely fulfil.

## Viewing availability

Navigate to **Billboards → [Billboard Name] → Availability** to open the calendar for a specific billboard.

The calendar uses colour coding to show the status of each date at a glance:

| Colour | Status | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| 🟢 Green | Available | The date is open and can be booked. |
| 🟠 Orange | Pending | An advertiser has submitted a booking request for this date, awaiting your approval. |
| ⬜ Grey | Blocked | You have manually blocked this date (e.g. for maintenance). |
| 🔴 Red | Booked | A booking has been confirmed and the date is reserved. |

## Blocking dates

Block dates to reserve them for maintenance, inspections, or any personal hold — blocked dates cannot be booked by advertisers.

1. Click any open (green) date on the calendar.
2. Select **Block Date(s)** from the menu that appears.
3. Choose a **start date** and **end date** to define the block range.
4. Optionally add a **note** describing the reason for the block (visible only to you).
5. Click **Save Block**. The selected dates turn grey on the calendar.

## Unblocking dates

To make blocked dates available again, click any grey (blocked) date on the calendar and click **Remove Block**. The dates immediately return to available (green) status.

## How bookings affect availability

When an advertiser submits a booking request for your billboard, the requested dates immediately show as **Pending** (orange) on your calendar. No other advertiser can submit an overlapping request while a pending request is active.

Once you approve the booking, those dates change to **Booked** (red) and are fully reserved. No additional bookings can be made for those dates. If you decline a pending request, the dates revert to **Available** (green) and can be requested again.

<Warning>
  Blocked dates cannot be booked even if an advertiser requests them. Always unblock dates before accepting bookings for that period.
</Warning>
