This guide walks you through everything you need to get up and running with AddtoZ — from creating your account to making your first booking. By the end, you'll have a configured profile, at least one billboard listed, and a confirmed booking on the calendar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Navigate to [addtoz.com](https://addtoz.com) and click **Sign Up**. Fill in your full name, email address, and a secure password, then click **Create Account**. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link to activate your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    After logging in, go to **Settings → Profile**. Fill in your business name, contact details, and billing information, then click **Save Changes**. Completing your profile ensures that invoices and client communications display accurate information.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first billboard">
    Click **Billboards** in the left sidebar, then click **Add Billboard**. Enter the billboard's name, full address, and dimensions (width × height in feet or metres). Set a price per day, then upload a clear photo of the billboard location. Click **Save Billboard** to publish it to your inventory.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a campaign">
    Go to **Campaigns → New Campaign**. Give the campaign a descriptive name and set the start and end dates. Optionally, enter a total budget to keep spend on track. Click **Create Campaign** to save it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Book your first slot">
    Go to **Bookings → New Booking**. Select the billboard you want to advertise on, then choose your desired dates from the availability calendar. Attach the campaign you created in the previous step, review the booking summary, and click **Confirm Booking** to complete the reservation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you are an advertiser rather than a billboard operator, you can skip Step 3 and go directly to Step 4 after completing your profile.
</Note>
