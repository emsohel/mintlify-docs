## What you can do with AddtoZ

AddtoZ is built for two types of users:

- **Billboard Operators** — list and manage your billboard inventory, set pricing, upload location photos, and accept bookings from advertisers.
- **Advertisers** — browse available billboards, book slots for your campaigns, upload artwork, and monitor campaign performance.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [addtoz.com](https://addtoz.com) and verify your email address. See [Account Setup](/account-setup) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first billboard">
    Navigate to **Billboards → Add Billboard** to add your first location with photos, dimensions, and pricing. See [Adding Billboards](/billboards/adding-billboards).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a campaign">
    Go to **Campaigns → New Campaign** to set up your first advertising campaign. See [Creating Campaigns](/campaigns/creating-campaigns).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book a slot and go live">
    Create a booking, upload your artwork, and approve the booking to go live. See [Creating Bookings](/bookings/creating-bookings).
  </Step>
</Steps>
