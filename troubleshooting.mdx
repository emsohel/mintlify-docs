If you're running into issues with AddtoZ, this page covers solutions to the most common problems. For issues not listed here, contact support at support@addtoz.com.

## Login & Account

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't log in to my account">
    Work through the following steps in order:

    1. Check that Caps Lock is off.
    2. Try resetting your password.
    3. Clear your browser cache and cookies.
    4. Try an incognito/private window.

    If you still can't log in, contact support with your account email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I didn't receive a verification email">
    1. Check your spam/junk folder.
    2. Make sure you entered the correct email address.
    3. Click **Resend verification email** on the login page.

    If the email still doesn't arrive after 10 minutes, contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billboards

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My billboard isn't showing on the map">
    1. Verify the billboard status is set to **Active**.
    2. Check that a valid address is entered and the map pin is correctly placed.
    3. If the address was recently added to a new development, drag the pin manually to the correct location.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The availability calendar isn't loading">
    1. Refresh the page.
    2. Try a different browser.

    If the issue persists for more than a few minutes, check the AddtoZ status page or contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Media Uploads

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My artwork upload is failing">
    1. Check the file size (max 100 MB).
    2. Verify the file format is supported — JPEG, PNG, or PDF for static; MP4 or GIF for digital.
    3. Ensure your internet connection is stable — large files require an uninterrupted upload.
    4. If the upload fails repeatedly, try compressing the file.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My uploaded artwork looks distorted in the preview">
    The preview renders at a reduced resolution. The final printed or displayed version will use your original file. If you need to check exact dimensions, download the file from the booking's **Artwork** tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Bookings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I submitted a booking but haven't heard back">
    1. Check the booking status in **Bookings**.
    2. If it shows **Pending** for more than 3 business days, use the booking's message thread to follow up with the operator.
    3. If the operator is unresponsive, contact AddtoZ support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My booking was declined — what should I do?">
    Declined bookings include a reason from the operator. Common reasons include:

    - **Dates unavailable** — try different dates.
    - **Minimum booking duration not met** — check the billboard's minimum booking requirement.
    - **Operator not accepting new bookings** — try a different billboard.

    Adjust your dates or select an alternative billboard and submit a new booking request.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My payment failed">
    1. Check that your card details are up to date in **Settings → Billing**.
    2. Ensure the billing address matches your card.
    3. Try a different payment method.
    4. If your card is still being declined, contact your bank — some banks block online payments by default.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I was charged but the booking still shows as unpaid">
    Payment processing can take a few minutes. Refresh the page after 5 minutes. If the booking is still **Unpaid** after 30 minutes and your bank shows the charge, contact support with your transaction reference number.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still need help?

If none of the above solutions resolve your issue, reach out to AddtoZ support:

- **Email:** support@addtoz.com
- Use the **Help** button in the bottom-right corner of any AddtoZ page
