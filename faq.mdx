Find quick answers to the most common questions about AddtoZ below. If you don't find what you're looking for, visit the Troubleshooting page or contact support.

## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is AddtoZ free to use?">
    AddtoZ offers a free plan for small operators (up to 3 billboard listings). Paid plans unlock unlimited listings, advanced analytics, and priority support. See the Pricing page on addtoz.com for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to create an account to browse billboards?">
    You need a free account to view detailed billboard information, availability calendars, and pricing. Browsing the map overview is available without an account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use AddtoZ as both an operator and an advertiser?">
    Yes. A single AddtoZ account can manage billboard inventory (operator) and create campaigns and bookings (advertiser) simultaneously.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billboards

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many billboards can I list?">
    On the free plan, you can list up to 3 billboards. Paid plans allow unlimited listings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I temporarily hide a billboard without deleting it?">
    Yes. Set the billboard's status to **Inactive** in the billboard settings. It will be hidden from advertisers but all its data is preserved.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What image formats are accepted for billboard photos?">
    Listing photos must be JPEG or PNG, maximum 10 MB each. For artwork uploads, see the [Media Upload](/billboards/media-upload) page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Bookings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does a booking approval take?">
    Operators typically respond within 24–48 hours. If you haven't received a response after 3 business days, you can send a follow-up message through the booking thread.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I modify a booking after it's been approved?">
    Date changes on approved bookings require the operator's agreement. Open the booking and click **Request Change** to send a modification request.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if an operator cancels my approved booking?">
    If an operator cancels an approved booking, you receive a full refund automatically. AddtoZ will also notify you and suggest alternative billboards in the same area.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When am I charged for a booking?">
    Payment is due when the operator approves your booking and an invoice is generated. Payment must be completed before your campaign can go live.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    AddtoZ accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and bank transfers. See [Payments](/bookings/payments) for setup instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund if I cancel?">
    Refund eligibility depends on the operator's cancellation policy and how far in advance you cancel. Check the billboard listing for cancellation terms before booking.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account & Settings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    On the login page, click **Forgot password?**, enter your email address, and follow the link in the email you receive.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple users on one account?">
    Yes. Go to **Settings → Team** to invite team members. See [Account Setup](/account-setup) for role descriptions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
