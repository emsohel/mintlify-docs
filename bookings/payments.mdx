AddtoZ processes payments securely for all billboard bookings. This page explains how to pay invoices, add and manage payment methods, and review your payment history.

## Add a payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Billing">
    Open **Settings** from the left sidebar and select **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Payment Method">
    Click **Add Payment Method** to open the payment setup form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your payment details">
    Enter your card details (Visa, Mastercard, and American Express are accepted) or select **Bank Transfer** to set up a bank account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your payment method">
    Click **Save**. Your payment method is now available for future invoices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  AddtoZ uses industry-standard encryption for all payment data. Your card details are never stored on AddtoZ servers.
</Note>

## Pay an invoice

To pay an outstanding invoice:

1. Go to **Billing → Invoices** and locate the invoice with an **Unpaid** status.
2. Click **Pay Now** on the invoice.
3. Select your preferred payment method from the list of saved methods.
4. Click **Confirm Payment** to complete the transaction.

After a successful payment, you will receive a payment receipt by email and the invoice status will update to **Paid**.

<Warning>
  Unpaid invoices may result in your booking being cancelled. Pay invoices promptly to ensure your campaign goes live as scheduled.
</Warning>

## Payment history

All past payments are listed in **Billing → Payment History**. Each entry shows:

- **Date** — when the payment was processed
- **Amount** — the total amount charged
- **Invoice number** — the reference number for the associated invoice
- **Status** — whether the payment was successful, pending, or failed

Use this section to reconcile payments with your finance records or to re-download receipts for any completed transaction.

## Automatic payments

To avoid manually paying each invoice, enable auto-pay in AddtoZ:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Enable Auto-Pay**.
3. Confirm your default payment method.

With auto-pay enabled, AddtoZ automatically charges your default payment method as soon as a new invoice is issued for an approved booking. You will still receive an email notification each time a payment is processed.

To disable auto-pay at any time, return to **Settings → Billing** and click **Disable Auto-Pay**.

## Refunds and cancellations

Refund eligibility depends on two factors: how far in advance you cancel the booking, and the individual operator's cancellation policy. Each billboard listing displays the operator's cancellation policy — review it carefully before booking.

When a refund is approved:

- The refunded amount is returned to your original payment method.
- Processing typically takes **5–10 business days**, depending on your bank or card provider.
- You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been initiated.

To initiate a cancellation and request a refund, see [Cancel a booking](/bookings/creating-bookings#cancel-a-booking).
