AddtoZ automatically generates an invoice when a booking is approved, making it easy to track spend, share invoices with your finance team, and maintain records of all advertising purchases.

## How invoices are generated

When an operator approves your booking, AddtoZ immediately creates an invoice for that booking. No manual action is required on your part. Each invoice includes the following details:

- **Booking dates** — the start and end dates of your billboard reservation
- **Billboard name and location** — the specific billboard covered by the invoice
- **Price per day** — the daily rate agreed at the time of booking
- **Total days** — the full duration of the booking period
- **Subtotal** — price per day multiplied by the total number of days
- **Applicable taxes** — any taxes applied based on your billing region
- **Total due** — the final amount payable

## View invoices

You can access invoices in two ways:

- **Per booking:** Go to **Bookings → [Booking Name] → Invoice** tab to view the invoice for a specific booking.
- **All invoices:** Go to **Billing → Invoices** to see a consolidated list of all invoices across every booking in your account.

## Download an invoice

On any invoice page, click **Download PDF** to save a print-ready copy of the invoice to your device. Downloaded invoices include your business name and billing address as saved in your profile — make sure your profile details are up to date before downloading invoices for official use.

<Tip>
  Download invoices for your records as soon as a booking is approved — they include all the information your finance team needs for expense reporting.
</Tip>

## Invoice statuses

Each invoice in AddtoZ carries one of four statuses:

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | The invoice is being prepared. This is rare, as invoices typically auto-finalize upon booking approval. |
| **Unpaid** | The invoice has been issued and payment is pending. |
| **Paid** | Payment has been received and the invoice is settled. |
| **Void** | The invoice was cancelled, for example because the associated booking was cancelled. |

## Disputing an invoice

If you notice an error on an invoice — such as incorrect dates, pricing, or billing details — take one of the following steps:

- **Contact the operator directly** through the messaging thread on the booking page.
- **Raise a support dispute** by going to **Help → Support** and submitting a dispute request with the invoice number and a description of the issue.

AddtoZ's support team will work with you and the operator to resolve any discrepancies as quickly as possible.
