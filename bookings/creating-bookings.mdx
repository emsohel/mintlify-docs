A booking in AddtoZ reserves a specific billboard for a defined date range. This guide walks you through the full booking process — from selecting a location to getting approved and going live.

## Create a booking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a billboard">
    Browse the billboard map at **Billboards** or search by location. Click a billboard to view its availability calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check availability">
    On the billboard's availability calendar, confirm your desired dates are open (shown in green).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Book Now">
    On the billboard detail page, click **Book Now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select dates">
    Choose your start and end dates from the date picker. The system will show the total price based on Price per day × days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a campaign">
    Select an existing campaign or create a new one to associate this booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add booking notes">
    Optionally add notes for the operator (e.g., special requirements).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit booking request">
    Click **Submit Request**. The operator will review and approve or decline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After submitting

Once you submit your booking request, AddtoZ sends you an email confirmation. The operator has up to 3 business days to review and respond. During this time, your booking appears with a **Pending** status. You can track the status of all your bookings at any time by navigating to **Bookings** in the left sidebar.

If the operator approves your request, you will receive an approval confirmation email and the booking status updates to **Approved**. If the operator declines, you will also be notified by email and can search for an alternative billboard.

<Note>
  Booking requests are not confirmed until the operator explicitly approves them. Do not schedule your campaign launch until you receive an approval confirmation email.
</Note>

## View and manage bookings

Navigate to **Bookings** in the left sidebar to see all your bookings grouped by status:

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Submitted and awaiting operator review |
| **Approved** | Operator has confirmed the booking |
| **Live** | Your campaign is currently running |
| **Completed** | The booking period has ended |
| **Cancelled** | The booking was cancelled by you or the operator |

Click any booking to view its full details, including dates, pricing, attached campaign, and any messages from the operator.

## Cancel a booking

To cancel an existing booking:

1. Go to **Bookings** and find the booking you want to cancel.
2. Open the booking and click **Cancel Booking**.
3. Review the cancellation details and click **Confirm Cancellation**.

Cancellation policies vary by operator and are displayed on each billboard's listing page. Review the operator's policy before cancelling, as some bookings may be non-refundable or subject to a cancellation fee.
